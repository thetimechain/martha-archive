# What we don't know yet

This archive prefers to say what it does not know.

## Recently resolved

- **Martha Stewart Living Hallmark era (S10–S11, 2002–2004).** Augmented from marthastewart.tv on 2026-05-21: **S10 now lists 132 documented broadcasts (was 10)** and **S11 lists 104 (was 8)**. Most of these were "unretrievable" until we discovered Martha's own streaming archive had thumbnails and broadcast codes for them. Titles for the remaining segment-level entries are the official MSL broadcast codes (e.g. *MSL Season 10 Episode 175V*); descriptive titles surface where marthastewart.tv provided one.
- **Martha Stewart Living S9 (2001–2002).** Expanded from 68 to 174 documented broadcasts via the same source.
- **Martha & Snoop Season 3.** Confirmed: all 10 episodes (Apr 2019 – Feb 2020), season retitled *Potluck Party Challenge*.

## Remaining gaps

- **From Martha's Kitchen** (Food Network, 2000–2004): 52 of 100 episodes documented. The remaining ~48 appear unretrievable — TheTVDB, Yidio, IMDb, TV Guide, and marthastewart.tv all show only the same 35 S1–S5 entries plus the expanded S6.
- **The Martha Stewart Show** (2005–2012): 1,216 broadcast days documented as calendar entries, but only ~110 have titles. The rest are placeholder date-only rows. Public databases offer no further detail.

## Method

Our two sources are the original `episodes.json` seed (compiled from TheTVDB, IMDb, TVGuide, Yidio, and Wikipedia) and marthastewart.tv itself (the official Vimeo OTT archive at https://www.marthastewart.tv). Augmented rows are labelled with `provenance = 'marthastewart-tv'` so the source of each title and image is auditable.
